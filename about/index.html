We build the calm
part of money.

A tarn is a small, deep mountain lake. Still on the surface, clear all the way down. That's how we want your finances to feel when you open them.

Clear before
clever

We say what we see in words anyone can check, with the numbers beside it.

On your side,
not on commission

You pay us. Tarn earns nothing from the products it points to.

Read it,
never touch it

We see your accounts so we can help. We never move your money.

Most money apps show you more charts. We think you need fewer, and one clear sentence about what to do next. So we started with a small team, a quiet interface and a rule: if it takes more than a sentence to explain, it isn't ready.
