Questions, answered

Does Tarn sort my spending automatically?

Yes. Each transaction gets a category as it arrives. If one is wrong, change it once and Tarn applies the fix to similar purchases.

How does Tarn find my subscriptions?

It looks for charges that repeat on a pattern, such as the same merchant and amount each month, and lists them with the next renewal date.

Can I set my own categories?

Yes. Rename, merge or add categories, and Tarn will use yours from then on.

Does anyone else see my spending?

No. Tarn reads your accounts to help you and doesn't sell your data. On a Household plan, your partner sees only what you choose to share.
