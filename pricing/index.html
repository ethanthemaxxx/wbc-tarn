Questions, answered

Can Tarn move my money?

No. Tarn connects in read-only mode, so it can see balances and transactions but can't spend, transfer or trade. Any action is yours to take.

Is the Free plan really free?

Yes. It has no card requirement and no end date. It connects two accounts and sends a weekly note, and you can upgrade whenever you want more.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You can export it before you go. After cancellation Tarn disconnects your accounts and deletes your data within 30 days.

Who is the Household plan for?

Two adults who share money, such as partners or co-parents. Each person keeps private accounts private and chooses what the other can see.
